Questions

How do I access the voice guidance after subscribing?

After your payment is confirmed, ThriveCart sends you a welcome email containing your monthly access code. Enter it in the "Already subscribed?" box above. Your access is then saved on this device — no account or login needed.

How does the monthly code work?

Each month, a new access code is emailed to active subscribers. Simply enter the new code when prompted. If you cancel your subscription you will not receive future codes, and the previous month's code will stop working at the next rotation.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Access is device-based using a simple code. Enter your code once and voice guidance is active on that device for the month. If you switch devices, just enter your code again.

What audio files are used?

Voice guidance is played as MP3 files directly in your browser. Playback requires a data connection on the first load; after that, most browsers cache the files automatically.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time via ThriveCart. You retain access until the end of your current billing period.
