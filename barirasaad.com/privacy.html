Privacy notice

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what happens to the information you send me through the enquiry form on this site, and what rights you have over it. I have tried to write it in plain English rather than legal boilerplate.

Who is responsible for your information

The data controller is Barira Saad, operating as a sole trader in the United Kingdom. You can reach me at hello@barirasaad.com.

[[CONFIRM]] Add your trading address if you are required to publish one, or state that it is available on request.

What I collect, and why

The only information this site collects is what you type into the enquiry form:

  • Your name — so I know who I am replying to.
  • Your organisation or company (optional) — so I can understand the context of the enquiry.
  • Your email address — so I can reply.
  • Your description of the project — so I can tell you whether I can help, and quote sensibly.

I use this information for one purpose only: to respond to your enquiry and, if we go ahead, to carry out the work. I do not use it for marketing, I do not build a mailing list from it, and I do not sell or share it with anyone for their own purposes.

Please don’t include confidential or patient information in your message. If you send clinical or personal details about someone else, I will delete them and ask you to resend without them.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. It loads no fonts, scripts or images from third-party servers, so simply reading the page does not send your details anywhere.

[[CONFIRM]] If you later add analytics, embedded video, a chat widget or a web font hosted elsewhere, this paragraph must be updated and you may need a cookie banner.

My lawful basis for using it

Under the UK GDPR I need a lawful basis for handling your information. I rely on:

  • Steps to enter into a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) — where your enquiry is about commissioning work from me, and replying is part of arriving at an agreement.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) — for general enquiries and for keeping a record of correspondence about my freelance practice. My interest is in running that practice; I have weighed it against your privacy and consider the impact minimal, because the information is limited to business contact details you chose to send me.

Who else sees it

The enquiry form is handled by Formspree, which receives your submission and forwards it to my email inbox. Formspree acts as a processor on my behalf. Their own privacy notice is at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

[[CONFIRM]] Formspree is US-based, so submissions may be transferred outside the UK. Confirm the safeguards in place — the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, or the International Data Transfer Agreement / Addendum — and name the one you are relying on here. Confirm too which email provider hosts your inbox, since they also process the message.

Beyond that, I share your information only where I am legally required to — for example in response to a lawful request from a regulator or a court.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to work are deleted after 12 months [[CONFIRM]]. Where an enquiry becomes a commission, I keep the correspondence and project records for six years after the end of the work [[CONFIRM]], which reflects the usual period for tax records and for a contract claim to be brought.

Keeping it secure

This site is served over HTTPS, so what you type into the form is encrypted in transit. My email account is protected with a strong, unique password and two-factor authentication, and enquiry records are deleted on the schedule above.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information I hold about you;
  • have inaccurate information corrected;
  • ask me to delete it;
  • ask me to restrict how I use it;
  • object to my using it where I rely on legitimate interests — including the right to object at any time;
  • receive it in a portable, machine-readable format where the processing is based on contract or consent.

To exercise any of these, email me at hello@barirasaad.com. I will respond within one month. There is no charge.

Complaints

If you think I have handled your information badly, please tell me first and I will try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office, the UK supervisory authority, at ico.org.uk, or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice

If I change how I handle enquiries, I will update this page and change the date at the top.
